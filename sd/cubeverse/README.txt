CubeVerse keeps its settings, your Juxt sign-in and a cache of pictures in
this folder. account.ini holds your Pretendo Network ID and password,
scrambled, so CubeVerse can sign you in again by itself; anyone with this SD
card could recover them. Signing out in Settings deletes it.
cacert.pem lets CubeVerse talk to Juxt and GitHub securely. Keep it here.
